I went to Japan with [Sara](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sara-zambrini-21b87a191/) (btw she wrote this article) in 2025, after visiting Bali and Singapore. We had been dreaming of visiting Japan for so long that we couldn't believe it was happening. After years of waiting, that day finally arrived.

We landed in Osaka at the beginning of September and were immediately hit by an unbelievable heat, unusual for this time of year. After our first stop at a sushi bar, the night became unexpectedly fun when three Japanese guys joined us for drinks, food, and then even more drinks.

## Osaka

In the following days, we explored several areas of the city: Dotonbori, Osaka Castle, and the fish market, one of the places we loved the most. Osaka was one of the best cities we had ever seen, full of lights, markets, incredible street food like takoyaki, and kawaii vibes everywhere.

## Day trips

We took several day trips out of Osaka.

**Nara** — deer walking freely through the streets, temples, and a calm that felt completely out of time.

**Kobe** — a slow day at the beach followed by an unforgettable experience: tasting real Kobe beef. Worth every yen.

**Kyoto** — one of the best spicy ramen we've ever had, in a small izakaya hidden among the city streets. We also caught the Yokai Festival, which was something else entirely.

## Tokyo

Our final days were in Tokyo, arriving by Shinkansen. Shibuya Crossing was overwhelming in the best way. People, neon, skyscrapers stacked on top of each other.

We had the pleasure of trying an authentic omakase dinner and meeting Chef Lee. Every course was a different world. Coming back to Italy after that was genuinely sad.
