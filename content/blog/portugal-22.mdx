In September 2022, a couple of friends and I organized a week long trip to Portugal to visit a friend who had settled in Peniche. It was my first time in the country.

## Porto

We chose Porto as our first stop, a wonderful city where we immediately found ourselves drinking beers by the river and wandering the bars in the center. One of those evenings where you arrive somewhere and it just works.

## Peniche

The next day we took a bus to meet [Roberto](https://www.robertosalodini.com/) in Peniche. We spent a few days working remotely, starting mornings with _pastéis de nata_, and in the evenings trying sardines prepared in every way imaginable.

I remember dining at **Restaurante D Raiz**. It comes highly recommended.

## Lisbon

To make the most of the weekend we took a taxi to Lisbon. Accommodation was scarce and the place we found wasn't exactly amazing, but the location was impossibly central.

Lisbon was the best part of the trip. Packed with nightlife, breathtaking views, and more stairs than you'd expect. I'll never forget **Santa Clara dos Cogumelos** — the entire menu was mushroom based. The atmosphere was strange and fun, and the food was exceptional.
