In May 2022, three friends and I organized a two-week remote work trip to Morocco. Our destination was Taghazout, with stops in Paradise Valley, Agadir, and Tamraght. My friends were determined to surf, and that was the best area in Morocco for it.

I was pleasantly surprised by the Moroccan culinary scene, we had couscous and tagine for lunch almost every day, interspersed with grilled fish and tacos. We also visited incredible local markets and spent evenings at local spots looking for available alcohol.

We stayed at the [Adventurekeys Surf Camp - Coworking Space](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Adventurekeys+Surf+Camp+-+Coworking+Space+-+Yoga+Retreat/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0xa7a552d2478f44a?sa=X&ved=1t:2428&ictx=111), where we could work from a rooftop overlooking the sea and enjoy impeccable service.

We spent our weekends by the ocean surfing, paddleboarding, swimming, exploring the desert and having a great time getting to know the locals.
