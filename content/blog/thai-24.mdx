My first time in Thailand was during 2013 and it blew my mind. I've been many times more after that first one, the people there are the most kind and funny ever and every time I must go back to Koh Phangan, at least some days, it is my favourite island.

During 2024 my brother went back to Italy for vacations and before going back to Australia we have been together in Thailand. Starting from Bangkok where we had too much fun and parties around the city, passing from Chatuchak market, Sukhumvit soi, Lumphini Park, China town and Khao San Road.

After that we needed some relax, so we opted for Chiang Mai, where we could enjoy the delicious local food and best spa ever. I also went to some beautiful temples in the jungle, the Tiger Temple and the Muay Thai stadium.

Then my brother finished the holidays and I moved with Sara for one night in Surat Thani, a small town known for its pier. From there we took the ferry to reach Koh Phangan first and Koh Samui later. Not looking for the touristic full moon party there, I don't like Phangan for that, I love to drive the bike through its breathtaking roads and explore the jungle, the night markets and some peculiar jungle parties.

Koh Samui was a bit too crowded for me but still very nice, there are still some natural places moving inland, and the airport there is one of the most simple but beautiful.
