When I started working for Palmabit, now [Webidoo Engineering](https://www.webidooengineering.com/) in 2020, I secured a fully remote role and immediately set off for the Canary Islands with two friends. Upon arriving in Tenerife, we teamed up with other remote workers, forming a group that grew in size and closeness over time.

As pandemic restrictions came into effect, we moved from island to island, experiencing one of the most fun periods of our lives.

## Tenerife

We traveled from the south to the north of the island, exploring every corner at our own pace. The variety was striking: volcanic landscapes in the morning, ocean at sunset.

We went on numerous excursions: hiking Mount Teide, trekking through the mountains, countless boat trip and discovering natural pools tucked along the coastline.

## Gran Canaria

We spent Christmas in a _finca_, a country estate that was entirely at our disposal. It felt like a different world: quiet, warm, surrounded by banana plantations, _vino tinto_ and open fields.

## Fuerteventura

New Year's Eve in Fuerteventura closed the loop. Private parties on an island that feels like a desert drifted into the Atlantic: endless dunes, flat horizons, wind.

The friendships from those months endure to this day, we often meet up or travel together in various countries around the world.

In the years that followed, we also met up several times in the Canary Islands working remotely together and managing reunions parties celebrating various birthdays that fell close together.
