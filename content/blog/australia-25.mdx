My first time in Australia was in 2013, I moved there with my dear friend [Toby](https://www.instagram.com/tobym.music/). We moved to Melbourne to work and travel, improving our English and experiencing really fun times together. During those times I remember I faced many challenges, I worked as a waiter, dishwasher and container unloader. I realized my skills were superficial and getting the sponsorship visa was not so easy as I thought. Anyway after almost one year I was very satisfied with everything I experienced and learned.

In May 2025 I went back there to meet [my brother](https://www.chefakela.com/) who is now an official Australian citizen.
I was so happy and excited to spend some months together and see Melbourne again after more than 10 years.

Following the Italian time zone was a bit hard, I used to work from 17.00 to 2.00 am every day, but it was worth it. Every weekend I was able to help my brother with his events or go around the city and more. We did some great nights together, played a lot with his PlayStation and VR mask, did some crazy Super Mario Go-Kart and did skiing in Mount Buller as well.
