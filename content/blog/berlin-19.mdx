I’ve been to Berlin many times, but the first time I managed to work remotely there was during the 2019 Christmas season. My very good friend [Marvin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/marvin-artioli/) had moved there permanently to study coding, and at the time, we had both just started working as developers.

Although the era of fully remote work hadn’t quite arrived yet, I managed to tack a few days of remote work onto my Christmas holidays and took the opportunity to spend about twenty days with my friend in Europe’s craziest city.

I remember that back then I was still working with jQuery, and we often discussed how the frontend world was shifting en masse to React.

We spent Christmas Eve at the Griessmühle club, partying until Christmas morning, then headed to the ice rink at Alexanderplatz before wrapping things up with a quieter dinner at a friend’s place.

It was an unforgettable New Year’s celebration that began on December 31st and ended on January 2nd, moving from club to club and meeting so many unique people.

I remember that when I caught my flight back to Milan in mid-January, the first cases of Covid were starting to emerge, and the first fever checks were being implemented at airports.

About once a year, I go back there to visit my friend Marvin.
