In September 2023, a few friends and I gifted a flight to Turkey to another friend, ensuring we could spend two weeks combining work and vacation together.

After landing in Istanbul, we rented a car and set off for Bursa, where we stayed for a couple of days. Our next stop was Izmir; we had a fantastic time there, thanks to a local guy who showed us the city's nightlife.

Continuing south, we arrived in Bodrum and rented a stunning villa with a sea view. Several days of beach time, delicious kebabs, and seafood dinners followed before we headed back north toward Istanbul, making stops along the way in small villages like Gölyazı Merkez.

Our final days in Istanbul were the most intense — both in terms of the daily distance covered and the amount of alcohol consumed — but perhaps also the most fun. Local culture, bazaars, rooftop bars, and nightclubs were the highlights of our time in the city.
