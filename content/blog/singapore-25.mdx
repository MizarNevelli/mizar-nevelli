When Sara and I booked the flight from Bali to Osaka, we chose a long layover in Singapore, enough time to actually see the city. The flight times worked out perfectly: we arrived in the morning and left the following night.

## Changi

One of the reasons we stopped here was Changi Airport, the most incredible airport we've ever seen. The world's tallest indoor waterfall right in front of your eyes. At night there's even a light show. The whole thing is surrounded by a tropical forest with walking paths and actual vegetation, all inside a terminal. Hard to believe until you're standing in it.

## The city

Outside it was hot and humid, obviously, but we walked around all day anyway.

In Chinatown we found one of the cheapest Michelin starred restaurants in the world, fresh ingredients, no fuss. The pork rib noodles and the soya chicken noodles are the ones to get.

**Gardens by the Bay** was something else: botanical beauty and futuristic architecture in the same place, unlike any garden we'd ever seen. Also full of mosquitoes starving for tourist blood.

We ended the day at Marina Bay, happy hour with a sunset view over the whole skyline, then street food around town to close it out.

Sixteen hours. Worth every minute.
